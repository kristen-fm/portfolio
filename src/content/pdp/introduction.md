# Contextual Statement

My professional experience has taken me from teaching English as a second language to software engineering, engineering leadership, and the design of technical learning experiences for software practitioners. Throughout these transitions, I've remained deeply interested in how adults learn, how people develop expertise, and how educational experiences can support professional and personal development.

I'm pursuing my M.Ed. in Learning Design and Training to deepen my understanding of adult education as both an academic discipline and a professional practice, and to develop the research and scholarly writing skills necessary to contribute to the field. I also hope to pursue doctoral study, with the longer-term goal of conducting original research related to adult learning and professional development.

This Professional Development Plan outlines the academic, scholarly, and professional activities I intend to pursue during my graduate program and immediately afterward. It emphasizes developing relationships within the adult education community, building research expertise, integrating scholarship with my professional practice, completing my degree, and preparing for doctoral study.
