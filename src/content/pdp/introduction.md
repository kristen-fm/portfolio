# Overview

This Professional Development Plan outlines the academic, community, and professional activities I intend to pursue over approximately the next four years — during and immediately after I complete my M.Ed. in Learning Design and Training at Colorado State University. The plan emphasizes developing relationships within the adult education community, building research expertise, integrating adult education and interdisciplinary scholarship with my professional practice, successfully completing my degree, and preparing for doctoral study.

I chose this particular set of goals because they reflect my interests — for example, autoethnographic research methodology and beliefs about learning — and because they represent actions I believe will facilitate my entry into the adult education community as a scholar-practitioner.

Taken together, I hope these goals let me do three things:

- *Know the field well enough to place my own research questions within it*. This will require me to read current scholarship deeply and critically; to understand the history and contemporary conversations around motivation, engagement, and beliefs about learning; and to write autoethnography.
- *Do research other people can use*. This will mean moving from coursework to conference proposals to peer-reviewed publication, as well as bringing what I learn back into the learning experiences I design for software practitioners, so that my scholarship and my practice feed each other.
- *Achieve a credible scholarly profile*. With a completed M.Ed., a record of presenting and publishing, and relationships with faculty who know my work well enough to speak for it, I hope to be a competitive candidate for the doctoral programs to which I eventually apply.
