# Goal 4 — Complete my M.Ed. in Learning Design and Training

*Complete degree requirements while building a meaningful body of reflective, scholarly, and instructional work.*

| ID and Specific Objective | Commitment | Actionable Steps | Resources | Timeline | Measure of Success | Also Supports |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **G4.1** Successfully complete all required and elective coursework | Core | Follow graduation plan; enroll in one course per semester; complete assignments and degree requirements; regularly review academic progress with advisor | [Graduation plan](https://docs.google.com/document/d/193sDsXSjT8YdrrbTt2Mp5E5TyArMNLhuom1sTbHcAF4/edit); CSU graduate catalog; assigned advisor; Canvas; Jira | Each semester through graduation | Complete all required credits and degree requirements; earn M.Ed. | G2, G5 |
| **G4.2** Build a body of reflective, scholarly, and instructional work throughout the program | Core | Write a weekly learning-journal reflection; develop one autoethnographic piece based on learning from each course; select, revise, and publish at least one course artifact to the professional portfolio | Course readings and assignments; M.Ed. journal; faculty feedback; Google Docs; professional portfolio website | Weekly reflections; autoethnography and artifact for each course | Complete weekly journal entries, one autoethnographic draft per course, and at least one published portfolio artifact per course | G2, G3 |
| **G4.3** Select and complete the thesis or portfolio culminating pathway | Core | Meet with assigned advisor to compare pathways; consider alignment with research interests and doctoral aspirations; select a pathway; develop a completion plan; fulfill pathway requirements | Assigned CSU advisor; M.Ed. degree requirements; thesis and portfolio guidelines; faculty mentors | Decide sufficiently early to plan appropriate coursework and milestones; complete by graduation | Make an informed pathway decision and successfully complete the required culminating work | G2, G5 |
