# Goal 1 — Become an active member of the adult education community

*Develop meaningful relationships with scholars and practitioners and begin contributing to the field.*

| ID and Specific Objective | Commitment | Actionable Steps | Resources | Timeline | Measure of Success | Also Supports |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **G1.1** Develop & sustain meaningful relationships with peers & professors | Core | Participate actively in courses; attend faculty office hours; initiate conversations about shared research interests; maintain relationships beyond individual courses | CSU faculty & advisor; graduate peers; CSU-hosted academic events | Ongoing | Establish & maintain relationships characterized by ongoing intellectual exchange, mentorship, or collaboration | G5 |
| **G1.2** Become an active participant in AAACE and the broader community | Core | Join AAACE at the student rate; subscribe to print editions of *Adult Education Quarterly* & *Adult Learning*; attend association events & professional development activities; explore opportunities for volunteer involvement | AAACE student membership with both print journals (\$55 per year); AAACEd It! webinars; association events; professional networks | Joined AAACE in Fall 2026; participation ongoing | Maintain membership & participate meaningfully in association activities each year | G2 |
| **G1.3** Develop and disseminate scholarly work | **Core:** Conference presentation. **Stretch:** Journal publication | Identify a research topic; review calls for proposals; prepare & submit a proposal; present if accepted; evaluate selected presentations for manuscript development; develop & submit a manuscript | AAACE annual conference; faculty mentors; proposal guidelines; *Adult Education Quarterly*; *Adult Learning*; *Journal of Transformative Education*; CSU library | AAACE conference: Every October; manuscript development during or immediately after program | Submit at least one AAACE proposal each year; present when accepted; develop at least one presentation into a manuscript & submit it for publication | G2, G5 |
