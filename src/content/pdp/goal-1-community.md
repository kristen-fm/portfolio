# Goal 1 — Become an active member of the adult and continuing education community

*Develop meaningful relationships with scholars and practitioners and begin contributing to the field.*

| ID and Specific Objective | Commitment | Actionable Steps | Resources | Timeline | Measure of Success | Also Supports |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **G1.1** Develop and sustain meaningful scholarly relationships with peers and professors | Core | Participate actively in courses; attend faculty office hours; initiate conversations about shared research interests; maintain relationships beyond individual courses | CSU faculty and assigned advisor; graduate peers; academic events | Each semester; ongoing | Establish and maintain relationships characterized by ongoing intellectual exchange, mentorship, or collaboration | G5 |
| **G1.2** Become an active participant in AAACE and the broader ACE community | Core | Join AAACE; attend association events and professional development activities; explore opportunities for volunteer involvement | AAACE membership; association events; professional networks | Join during first year; participate throughout program | Maintain membership and participate meaningfully in association activities each year | G2 |
| **G1.3** Develop and disseminate scholarly work through annual conference participation | **Core:** Annual proposal preparation and submission. **Stretch:** Journal publication | Identify a research topic; review the annual call for proposals; prepare and submit a proposal; present if accepted; evaluate selected presentations for manuscript development; submit at least one suitable manuscript | AAACE annual conference; faculty mentors; proposal guidelines; peer-reviewed journals; CSU library | Every October during program, with proposals prepared before each year's deadline; manuscript development during or immediately after program | Submit at least one AAACE proposal each year; present when accepted; develop at least one presentation into a manuscript and submit it for publication | G2, G5 |
