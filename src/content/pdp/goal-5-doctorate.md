# Goal 5 — Prepare and submit applications to doctoral programs

*Develop a competitive scholarly profile, identify programs and advisors aligned with my interests, and pursue admission to a Ph.D. or Ed.D. program.*

| ID and Specific Objective | Commitment | Actionable Steps | Resources | Timeline | Measure of Success | Also Supports |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **G5.1** Develop a competitive scholarly profile | Core | Review academic & scholarly accomplishments each semester; identify gaps in research experience, writing, & professional engagement; seek faculty feedback; maintain an academic CV & record of scholarly outputs | Assigned advisor; faculty mentors; academic CV examples; course & scholarly work; PDP | Review each semester throughout program | Maintain an up-to-date academic CV & portfolio; demonstrate progress in academic performance, scholarly writing, research experience, & professional engagement | G1, G2, G4 |
| **G5.2** Identify doctoral programs and potential advisors aligned with my scholarly interests | Core | Research relevant doctoral programs at CSU, UNC, CU Boulder, & online programs; review faculty publications & research; compare program structures, funding, & requirements; contact potential advisors as appropriate | University doctoral program websites; faculty publications; graduate coordinators; current doctoral students; CSU faculty mentors | Begin Spring 2027; finish by Fall 2028 | Develop a documented shortlist of programs & potential advisors with evidence of research fit | G1, G2 |
| **G5.3** Prepare and submit competitive doctoral applications | **Core:** Application preparation & submission. **Desired outcome:** Admission | Review admission criteria & deadlines; prepare academic CV, statements, & writing samples; request recommendations; revise application materials with feedback; submit applications; evaluate offers | Admissions guidelines; faculty mentors; potential recommenders; graduate program staff | Fall 2030 | Submit complete applications by their deadlines; if offered admission, select a program that aligns with my scholarly & professional goals | G2 |
