# Goal 3 — Integrate graduate learning with professional practice

*Apply insights from adult education scholarship to the design and delivery of learning experiences for software practitioners.*

| ID and Specific Objective | Commitment | Actionable Steps | Resources | Timeline | Measure of Success | Also Supports |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **G3.1** Share relevant adult education research and theory with professional colleagues | Core | Identify research or theoretical perspectives relevant to the team; develop a brown-bag session; facilitate discussion; gather informal feedback | Graduate coursework; scholarly literature; workplace colleagues; professional learning opportunities | One session per quarter; preparation during work hours | Deliver four brown-bag sessions per year connecting adult education scholarship to the team's work | G2 |
| **G3.2** Apply graduate learning to professional practice and share the results | Core | Identify opportunities to apply concepts from coursework to instructional design or technical learning; implement selected approaches when feasible; reflect on the experience; write and publish an account on the professional portfolio | Graduate coursework; work projects; colleagues; instructional design research; existing portfolio website | Ongoing; at least one published reflection or case study per semester | Publish at least one portfolio piece per semester examining an application of graduate learning to professional practice | G2, G4 |
