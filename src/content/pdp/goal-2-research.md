# Goal 2 — Develop expertise in scholarly research and writing

*Develop the theoretical, methodological, analytical, and writing competencies necessary to conduct and disseminate independent scholarly research.*

| ID and Specific Objective | Commitment | Actionable Steps | Resources | Timeline | Measure of Success | Also Supports |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **G2.1** Develop foundational competencies in educational research | Core | Complete IRB training; successfully complete research methods coursework; critically evaluate empirical studies; apply methodological concepts in course projects | M.Ed. research methods courses; CSU library; IRB training; academic advisor | Throughout entire program | Complete IRB training & required research methods coursework; demonstrate methodological competence through academic work | G4, G5 |
| **G2.2** Establish & refine a scholarly research agenda | Core | Read & synthesize literature on motivation, engagement, beliefs about learning, & other constructs that pique interest; read each new issue of my subscribed journals to track what the field is publishing; identify research questions of interest; discuss emerging interests with faculty; refine research focus over time | *Adult Education Quarterly*; *Adult Learning*; *Journal of Transformative Education*; *The Cambridge Handbook of the Learning Sciences* (Sawyer, 2022); CSU library databases; faculty scholarship; research mentors; academic conferences | Begin Fall 2026; aim to solidify by Spring 2028 | Articulate a focused research agenda in writing by Spring 2028 & use it to frame at least one conference proposal & my doctoral application materials | G5 |
| **G2.3** Develop proficiency in autoethnographic research | **Core:** Write informal autoethnography. **Stretch:** Write formal autoethnography for journal submission | Study autoethnographic theory & methodology; use concepts from readings to inform course-related writing; analyze published exemplars; solicit feedback from faculty mentors on drafts; revise selected manuscripts; investigate suitable journals | *The Routledge International Handbook of Autoethnography in Educational Research* (Anteliz et al., 2023); scholarly journals; faculty mentors; CSU library | Begin immediately; practice throughout program; develop submission-ready work in later semesters | Demonstrate increasing methodological sophistication in autoethnographic writing; develop & submit at least one manuscript for publication | G1, G4 |
| **G2.4** Establish a sustained scholarly writing practice | Core | Join CSU Grad Writes; participate in writing sessions; establish regular writing time; seek & apply feedback; practice writing for scholarly audiences | CSU Writes / Grad Writes; faculty & peer feedback; scholarly writing guides; writing workshops | Join early; maintain practice throughout program | Sustain a consistent writing practice; produce & revise scholarly writing for courses, conference proposals, & manuscripts | G1, G5 |
