# Goal 2 — Develop expertise in scholarly research and writing

*Develop the theoretical, methodological, analytical, and writing competencies necessary to conduct and disseminate independent scholarly research.*

| ID and Specific Objective | Commitment | Actionable Steps | Resources | Timeline | Measure of Success | Also Supports |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **G2.1** Develop foundational competencies in educational research | Core | Complete research ethics training; successfully complete research methods coursework; critically evaluate empirical studies; apply methodological concepts in course projects | CSU research methods courses; CSU library; CITI/IRB training where applicable; assigned advisor | Early to middle program | Complete research ethics training and required research methods coursework; demonstrate methodological competence through academic work | G4, G5 |
| **G2.2** Establish and refine a scholarly research agenda | Core | Read and synthesize literature on motivation, engagement, beliefs about learning, professionalism, and professional identity; identify promising questions and connections; discuss emerging interests with faculty; refine research focus over time | Peer-reviewed journals; CSU library databases; faculty scholarship; research mentors; academic conferences | Explore throughout program; articulate a focused direction before doctoral applications | Develop a written research-interest statement identifying principal constructs, relevant literature, and potential research questions | G5 |
| **G2.3** Develop proficiency in autoethnographic research | **Core:** Methodological study and writing. **Stretch:** Journal submission | Study autoethnographic theory and methodology; use concepts from readings to inform course-related writing; analyze published exemplars; solicit feedback; revise selected manuscripts; investigate suitable journals | *The Routledge International Handbook of Autoethnography in Educational Research*; scholarly journals; faculty mentors; CSU library | Begin immediately; practice throughout program; develop submission-ready work in later semesters | Demonstrate increasing methodological sophistication in autoethnographic writing; develop at least one manuscript for possible publication; submit if ready | G4, G1 |
| **G2.4** Establish a sustained scholarly writing practice | Core | Join CSU Grad Writes; participate in writing sessions; establish regular writing time; seek and apply feedback; practice writing for scholarly audiences | CSU Writes / Grad Writes; faculty and peer feedback; scholarly writing guides; writing workshops | Join early; maintain practice throughout program | Sustain a consistent writing practice; produce and revise scholarly writing for courses, conference proposals, and manuscripts | G1, G5 |
