Code Review Anxiety Workbook

The Code Review Anxiety Workbook is a free, self-guided resource that helps software developers recognize and manage the anxiety many people experience giving and receiving code review. Dr. Carol Lee and I adapted a scientifically tested, 90-minute workshop intervention — developed at the Developer Success Lab and evaluated in a randomized controlled trial — into a self-paced workbook that individuals or teams can work through on their own or in a “workbook club.”

Grounded in decades of cognitive-behavioral research on anxiety, the workbook guides readers through strategies for managing physiological stress responses and through cognitive restructuring: identifying, challenging, and reframing anxious thoughts to be more realistic and self-compassionate. I include it here because it represents evidence-based learning design in an authentic workplace context — translating a facilitated, research-backed intervention into a durable, widely accessible self-directed learning experience.
