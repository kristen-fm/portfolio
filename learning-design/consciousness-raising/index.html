    CR as a Way of Knowing

    Consciousness-raising is more than a method—it offers a different way of knowing. It values lived experience, collective meaning-making, and the knowledge that emerges when people speak, listen, and connect their stories to larger social patterns.

    In CR, lived experience is a valid and vital form of evidence. Personal stories are not seen as merely anecdotal, but as data that can reveal broader social patterns when shared and analyzed collectively.
