About

My background spans second language teaching, software engineering, Developer Experience, and learning design, with a consistent interest in how adults develop new knowledge and capabilities.

I currently work as a Technical Curriculum Developer at Datadog, where I design hands-on learning experiences that help technical practitioners build skills through authentic practice.

My scholarly interests center on adult learning, motivation and participation, self-directed learning, learner beliefs, and workplace learning. Across both research and practice, I am especially interested in how evidence about why and how adults learn can inform the design of learning environments that support meaningful, increasingly autonomous learning.
