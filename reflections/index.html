Your Software Engineers Do Need Communication Skills

This essay pushes back on the idea that software engineers (SEs) don't need communication skills by arguing that SEs communicate constantly, and that communication failures carry real costs. I make a case to appeal to engineers' analytic mindsets by teaching them to analyze rhetorical situations as they craft their communication.
