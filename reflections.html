Learn How to Code: Insights from the L2 Motivational Self System

Learning to code is hard, but it's achievable — and what often separates those who persist from those who give up is motivation. This reflection borrows Zoltán Dörnyei's L2 Motivational Self System from second language acquisition research and asks how the “ideal” and “ought-to” selves might explain who sticks with learning to program.
